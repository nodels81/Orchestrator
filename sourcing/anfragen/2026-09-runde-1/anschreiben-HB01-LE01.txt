My name is Björn, and I run Bellowerk Manufaktur, a small leather workshop in Hamburg, Germany. We make leather dog collars and leads by hand, in vegetable-tanned oiled leather and solid brass, and every new design spends a season on real working dogs before it ever goes on sale. Quality and honest materials matter to us more than speed or price, and we are looking for a factory partner who shares that standard and can grow with us over time.

We would like to start in two deliberate steps, not with a production order straight away.

Step 1 - prototypes and samples: one prototype each of our HB-01 collar (size M) and LE-01 lead, built to the attached drawings, plus leather swatches of vegetable-tanned, oiled pull-up leather (3.5-4.0 mm / 9-10 oz) in grey, dark brown, olive, cognac and black, and samples of the solid brass fittings you would use (buckle, D-ring, O-rings, swivel snap hook, Chicago screws).

Step 2 - once we approve the prototypes, please quote a small first batch: HB-01 in 10 pcs per size (S/M/L/XL, 40 pcs total) and LE-01 in 10 pcs, plus your tiered price at 50 and 100 pcs so we can see how price develops with volume.

We know 10 pcs per size is well below a normal minimum order, and that is intentional: we start small so the quality is proven before we commit to larger, planned reorders, and we are glad to pay a fair premium on the prototypes and this first batch to reflect that. If you have a sample or small-batch department, or can run a low starting quantity against a surcharge, please let us know.

Materials are fixed: solid brass hardware only (no zinc alloy, no plated steel), no stitching, no rivets, no plastic, no webbing. On LE-01, the hooks and rings are attached with a bleed knot - the strap is slit lengthwise and pulled whole through itself, never cut into strands - exactly as shown on drawing sheets 2 and 3.

Could you also tell us whether you make packaging in-house or with a partner (kraft bag, printed hangtag, carton, our logo), and the price and MOQ for that?

Every delivery, prototypes included, should come with full pattern files (PDF and DXF, all measurements) - please confirm this is standard for you.

Attached: HB-01 drawing, LE-01 drawings (3 sheets, incl. the bleed-knot steps), and one photo of each finished piece.

We would be grateful for your reply by Tuesday, 30 September 2026, ahead of the holiday. Thank you for your time - we look forward to hearing from you.

Kind regards,
Björn
