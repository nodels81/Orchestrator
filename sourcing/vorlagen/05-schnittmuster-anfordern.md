# 05 — Schnittmuster und Unterlagen anfordern

Gehört in jede PO als Lieferumfang. Falls die Fabrik zögert: Wir bezahlen die Ware, die Zeichnungen
sind unsere; das Schnittmuster dokumentiert nur das Goldmuster, das wir freigegeben haben.

---

**Subject:** Bellowerk – PO [Nr.] – pattern files and documents (part of delivery)

Hello [Name],

as agreed in the PO, please send with the shipment (or by e-mail before shipment):

1. **Pattern for each model and size** — PDF at 1:1 scale and DXF:
   - outline of every leather part
   - total length and width in mm
   - hole positions, hole size, hole pitch
   - fold marks and Chicago screw positions
   - ring positions for LE-01 (45 / 140 / 245 cm from hook A)
   - nameplate position
   - leather thickness noted on the drawing
2. Cutting list: parts per product with dimensions (mm) and leather thickness
3. Leather data sheet: tannery, tanning type (vegetable), thickness, colour codes
4. Brass hardware: alloy (e.g. HPb59-1 / CW617N), supplier, breaking-load figures
5. Test report REACH chromium VI (leather) — or confirm we may test one piece ourselves
6. Photos of every model before packing

Please confirm you will send items 1–6 with the order. Deadline for the files: [date].

Best regards, Björn

---

## Wenn der Lieferant "no pattern" sagt

Hello [Name],

we understand. Then please send:
1. A photo of each leather part laid flat on a cutting mat with a ruler, before assembly
2. The measurements you use for cutting (length, width, holes) as a simple table

That is enough for our documentation. Thank you.
