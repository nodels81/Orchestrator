# HS-01 — Leather Hand Loop, 50 cm (Tech Pack v1.1, 18 Sep 2026)

Attachments: `zeichnungen/HS-01-handschlaufe.png` (dimensioned drawing + Bill of Materials), photo
`bilder/hardware-karabiner-1zu1.jpg` (photo-confirmed, binding 1:1 reference for the snap).

## 1. Product

Short handle loop for dogs that walk close, or as a clip-on handle for LE-01/LE-02.
Closed loop of leather, circumference **50 cm**, with one solid brass swivel trigger snap.

## 2. Material and construction

- Leather: as HB-01 (veg-tan oiled cowhide, 3.5–4.0 mm), width **20 mm**.
- Cut length: 50 cm + fold at snap 5 cm + overlap of loop ends 5 cm = **60 cm**.
- Snap: swivel trigger snap solid brass, eye 20 mm, min. 2.0 kN.
- Loop closed by overlapping the two strap ends 50 mm (flesh to flesh) and fixing with 2 Chicago
  screws; the snap eye sits in a second fold (50 mm, 2 screws) opposite the overlap.
- Snap attachment preferably as on LE-01: fold 120 mm, 3-strand mystery braid 80 mm, 1 Chicago screw.
- Leather patch PATCH-01 PA-S on the overlap (default: yes).
- Edges bevelled and burnished. No stitching, no rivets.
