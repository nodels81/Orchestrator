# LE-01 — Adjustable Leather Lead, 3.00 m (Tech Pack v1.0, 9 Sep 2026)

Bellowerk Manufaktur, Hamburg, Germany. Contact: Björn (owner).
Attachments: `zeichnungen/LE-01-fuehrleine.png`, photo(s) from `bilder/LE-01-*.jpg`.

## 1. Product

Long lead, 3.00 m, **three-way adjustable, six working lengths**. Solid brass swivel trigger snap at
both ends, three welded solid brass O-rings fixed along the strap. Single-layer leather, no stitching,
no rivets, all folds fixed with Chicago screws. Nameplate PATCH-01 near hook A.

## 2. Material

| Part | Specification |
|---|---|
| Leather | Same as HB-01: vegetable-tanned, full grain, oiled pull-up cowhide, **3.5–4.0 mm**, width **20 mm** (option 25 mm for XL dogs). One continuous strap, no joints along the length. Colours olive, dark brown, cognac. |
| Snap hooks (2) | Swivel trigger snap ("swivel eye trigger snap hook"), solid brass, eye for 20 mm strap, min. breaking load **2.0 kN**, strong spring, swivel runs freely. |
| O-rings (3) | Welded, ground smooth, solid brass, inner Ø 25 mm, wire Ø 5 mm, min. 3 kN. |
| Screws | Chicago screws solid brass, head Ø 5 mm, post 8 mm, thread-locked. 2 per fold. |
| Nameplate | PATCH-01, 2 Chicago screws. |
| Edges | Bevelled, burnished, no paint. |

## 3. Dimensions (all measured from the inner edge of hook A eye, ±1 cm)

| Position | Item | Distance from hook A | Fold |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 cm | Hook A | 0 | strap through eye, fold 50 mm, 2 screws |
| **45 cm** | Ring 1 | 45 | strap through ring, fold 50 mm, 2 screws |
| **140 cm** | Ring 2 | 140 | same |
| **245 cm** | Ring 3 | 245 | same |
| 300 cm | Hook B | 300 | fold 50 mm, 2 screws |

Total length hook eye to hook eye: **300 cm** (+ hook bodies). Cut length of strap: 300 cm + 5 folds ×
5 cm = **325 cm**, plus 2 cm tolerance.

Rings are fixed in the strap (strap folds back around the ring and is screwed) — they do not slide.

Plus: full 300 cm when both hooks are used (two dogs / tie-out).

## 4. Construction

1. Each fold: strap around hook eye/ring, back 50 mm on the same side (flesh to flesh), 2 Chicago
   screws in line at 15 mm and 35 mm from the fold. Screw heads on grain side (outside).
2. Nameplate PATCH-01 at 8 cm from hook A, centred.
3. Strap ends cut straight, edges bevelled all along both sides.
4. No twist in the strap; all screw heads on the same side.
