## 1. Product

Same function as LE-01 (3.00 m, rings at 45 / 140 / 245 cm, swivel trigger snaps both ends, six
working lengths) but the strap is a **4-strand round braid, Ø 12 mm**, veg-tan oiled leather, with a
leather core. Hooks and rings are attached by **back-braiding** (splice), no stitching, no rivets,
no crimp sleeves. A Chicago screw may be added through the splice only if needed for safety — ask.

## 2. Braid

- 4 strands 4 mm × 2.2 mm + core strand 4 mm × 4 mm, vegetable-tanned oiled cowhide.
- Finished Ø 12 mm ±1 mm, even along the whole length; strands from one hide per lead (colour match).
- Splice at each hook/ring: braid passes through the eye, is back-braided 60 mm into itself, ends
  tucked and trimmed. Splice must hold **2.0 kN** without slipping.
- Rings must be fixed in position (no sliding): ring sits in a braided loop, tolerance ±1 cm.

## 3. Positions from hook A eye

Ring 1: 45 cm · Ring 2: 140 cm · Ring 3: 245 cm · Hook B: 300 cm. Same length table as LE-01.

## 4. Hardware

Swivel trigger snaps solid brass, eye Ø ≥ 14 mm round (for Ø 12 mm braid), min. 2.0 kN. O-rings
welded solid brass inner Ø 25 mm, wire 5 mm. Nameplate PATCH-01 on a small flat leather tab
(40 × 20 mm, 3.5 mm leather) spliced/screwed at 8 cm from hook A.

## 5. Quantity

Samples 2 pcs olive; then 60 pcs: olive 30 / dark brown 20 / cognac 10. Packing as LE-01.
