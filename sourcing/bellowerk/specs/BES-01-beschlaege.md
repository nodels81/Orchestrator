## 1. Material rule — applies to every part below

- **Solid brass throughout** (cast CuZn39Pb3 / HPb59-1, or forged CW617N). Not plated, not filled.
- Not acceptable: zinc die-cast ("zamak"), brass-plated steel or iron, stainless steel with brass
  coating, lacquered brass, painted brass.
- Unlacquered. The brass is meant to darken with use.
- Incoming check on every delivery: **magnet test** (brass is not magnetic — a magnet that sticks
  means plated steel), **weight per piece** against the golden sample (±5 %), file test on one piece
  per batch (same colour all the way through), and the breaking loads in the table.

## 3. Finish

Brushed or lightly polished, unlacquered, no sharp edges, welds ground flush and invisible after
finishing. Spring in the bolt snap: stainless or brass, strong enough that the bolt closes fully
by itself when released from any position.

## 4. Open points — Björn decides before the RFQ goes out

1. ~~D-ring or O-ring on the collar?~~ **Decided 15 Sep 2026: D-ring.** The photos of the older
   pieces show a round ring — they are reference for leather, patch and finish, **not for the ring**.
   Every collar with a screwed buckle fold (HB-01, HB-04) gets a welded D-ring; the flat side sits
   against the strap so the ring cannot rotate under the fold. The braided collar HB-02 keeps its
   O-ring: it is worked into the braid eye, where a D-ring would twist. Say so in the RFQ, because a
   factory that compares drawing and photo will otherwise copy the photo.
2. **Finish: bright or dark?** The lead snaps are bright polished brass. On the braided collar the
   ring and snap look dark, almost antique. If that is an aged finish rather than the light,
   we need to say so — "antique brass" is a different part number at every supplier.
3. **The brass charm (BES-01-F)** hangs on the finished pieces but is in no tech pack. Part of the
   product, an extra, or a giveaway?
4. **No shackles anywhere.** The fittings on the photos are swivel bolt snaps, not shackles —
   if you want a shackle (screw pin, D-shape) on any model, that is a new part.
5. Punched holes stay **plain, without eyelets** — confirmed on the photos, noted here so nobody
   "improves" it.

## 5. Sourcing — where solid brass hardware comes from

**Rule: the leather factory buys and fits the hardware.** We do not ship parts to China. But we
prescribe alloy, dimensions, weight and breaking load, and we send one European reference piece with
the sample order so the golden sample can be compared against real solid brass.

## 6. Questions for the leather factory's quotation

1. Who makes your brass hardware — in-house or a hardware supplier? Name and city.
2. Alloy statement per part (CuZn39Pb3 / HPb59-1 / CW617N) and **weight in grams per piece**.
3. Photo of buckle, ring, bolt snap and Chicago screw next to a ruler, before sampling.
4. Breaking-load test report per part, at the loads in §2.
5. Price per piece for each part at 100 / 300 / 1000 sets, so we can see what the hardware costs
   inside the unit price.
6. Can you supply antique-finish brass as well as natural? Photo of both.
