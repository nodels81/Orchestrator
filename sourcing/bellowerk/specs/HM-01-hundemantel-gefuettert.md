## 1. Product

Blanket-style dog coat covering the back from the withers to the base of the tail. Three layers:
waxed cotton shell, light polyester wadding, woven cotton check lining. High stand collar that rolls
over, closing at the throat. One adjustable belly strap (two on XL/XXL). Bound edges in a contrast
colour. Reinforced leash slit on the spine. For medium to large family and working dogs — **not**
for sighthounds (`markenbrief.md`).

## 5. Construction

1. **Layer build**: shell — wadding — lining. Baste the wadding to the lining with 3 long rows
   (they disappear under the binding), then treat as one. No quilting: the reference coat is flat.
2. **All outer edges bound** with the 30 mm bias binding, 8 mm finished, topstitched close to the
   edge. Binding is also the design line — contrast colour, even width, no joins on a corner.
3. **Collar**: shell + wadding + inner fabric, bound along the top edge, sewn to the neck edge of
   the body panel with a 10 mm seam, seam covered by the binding. The collar rolls over; it must
   stand when open. **2 brass snaps** at the throat, pitch 40 mm, giving 40 mm of neck adjustment.
4. **Chest closure**: the two front corners of the body panel meet under the throat and close with
   **1 brass snap**, so the coat sits before the belly strap is closed.
5. **Belly strap**: sewn to the **left** side at 300 mm behind the neck edge (L size), bartacked in
   a box-X. It crosses under the belly to the counter patch on the right side. **3 snaps** at 40 mm
   pitch = ±40 mm girth adjustment. XL/XXL get a second strap 180 mm further back.
6. **Leash slit**: 80 mm long, on the spine, 120 mm behind the neck edge. Cut, bound all round,
   **bartacked at both ends**. It must not tear when a leash pulls — this is the load point.
7. **Hind-leg cut-out**: 70 mm deep curve on the lower rear edge so the hind legs run free.
   Tail end rounded with a 35 mm notch on the fold so the tail sits clear.
8. **Contrast yoke** (optional, decorative — as on our photos): printed cotton panel on the
   shoulder, edges turned and topstitched, before the layers are joined.
9. **Waxed-cotton sewing rules**: no pins (holes stay) — clips only. Needle 100/16–110/18, stitch
   3.5 mm, PTFE or roller foot, no hot iron.
10. **Washing**: the coat must survive 30 °C machine wash without the wadding migrating. Care label:
    wash cold, no tumble dry, re-wax the shell.
