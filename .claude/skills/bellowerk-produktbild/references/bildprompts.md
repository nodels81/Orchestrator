# Bildprompts

Kopiervorlagen für erzeugte Produktbilder. Die gepflegte Fassung mit Kopierknöpfen liegt als
Artifact; diese Datei ist die Quelle, damit die Prompts auch ohne Browser greifbar sind.

## Zwei Regeln, die über die Trefferquote entscheiden

1. **Verhältnisse statt Millimeter.** Ein Bildmodell rechnet nicht in mm. „exactly double the width
   of the ends" wirkt, „50 mm" nicht. Genauso Anzahlen: „exactly five oval holes".
2. **Keine Gravur erzeugen lassen.** Jedes Modell schreibt Buchstabensalat auf den Patch, der auf den
   ersten Blick echt aussieht. In jeden Prompt gehört `no text, no logo`; die Gravur wird danach mit
   `bilder/PATCH-01-artwork.svg` eingesetzt.

## Prüfliste (in dieser Reihenfolge)

Genau fünf Löcher · Mitte doppelt so breit wie die Enden · Übergang weich, kein Knick · eine Lage
Leder (eine Kantenlinie, nicht zwei) · keine Naht, keine Niete · Messing warm-golden statt silbern ·
Patch sechsmal so lang wie hoch mit zwei Schrauben · Schnalle am Ende, D-Ring direkt dahinter.

Fällt ein Punkt durch, ist das Bild unbrauchbar — auch ein schönes.

## HB-01 · Hauptbild — flach von oben

```
Studio product photograph of a single leather dog collar lying flat on a smooth off-white surface, photographed from directly above, the whole collar in frame and parallel to the image edge.

The collar is cut from ONE piece of grey vegetable-tanned oiled leather ("washed stone" grey). Its two ends are narrow; towards the middle it widens smoothly over a long tangential curve to exactly DOUBLE the width of the ends, then narrows back the same way. The widened middle section spans about one third of the total length. Single layer of leather, about 4 mm thick: the collar is WIDER in the middle, never thicker. Total length about 60 cm, ends about 25 mm wide, middle about 50 mm wide.

On the widened middle lies a long narrow patch of cognac-brown leather, about six times longer than it is tall, held by one domed solid-brass screw at each end.

Left end: a solid brass roller buckle, a brass D-ring right behind it, a leather keeper. Right end: exactly five oval holes in one evenly spaced row.

All metal is solid brass, unlacquered, warm gold, lightly brushed. Leather edges bevelled and burnished. No stitching anywhere, no rivets, no visible glue.

Soft diffuse daylight from the upper left, one gentle shadow under the collar, natural leather grain visible, muted true colours. Photographic, not illustrated. No text, no logo, no watermark.
```

## HB-01 · Detail — Verbreiterung mit Patch

```
Close-up product photograph of the widened middle section of a grey leather dog collar, three-quarter angle, shallow depth of field, the background falling out of focus.

A long narrow cognac-brown leather patch is screwed onto the widened section with two domed solid-brass screws, one at each end. The grey leather is a single layer about 4 mm thick, cut in one piece; its edge is bevelled and burnished to a smooth rounded line. No stitching, no rivets, no perforations in this area.

Raking light from the side so the burnished edge and the natural grain of the oiled leather catch the light. Brass warm, unlacquered, slightly worn from use. Muted natural colours, soft daylight, photographic, no studio gloss. No text, no logo, no watermark.
```

## HB-01 · Am Hund getragen

```
Documentary photograph of a calm short-haired dog standing outdoors on a field path in soft late afternoon light, seen from the side at the dog's head height, sharp focus on the collar around its neck.

The collar is grey leather, narrow at the ends and about twice as wide across the front of the neck, where a long narrow cognac-brown leather patch is held by two brass screws. A solid brass roller buckle sits at the side of the neck. Brass warm and unlacquered.

Natural setting, warm low sun, shallow depth of field, honest reportage look, no studio lighting, no props. Colours muted. No text, no logo, no watermark.
```

## HB-01 · Farbreihe

```
Five leather dog collars of identical shape laid side by side on a neutral linen surface, photographed straight from above, evenly spaced, equal distance between them, all pointing the same way.

Each collar is narrow at both ends and exactly twice as wide in the middle, with a smooth curved transition, and carries a long narrow cognac-brown leather patch held by two domed brass screws. Solid brass roller buckles, warm unlacquered brass.

Colours from left to right: washed grey, dark brown, olive green, cognac, black. Soft even daylight, no harsh shadows, muted natural palette, catalogue look, photographic. No text, no logo, no watermark.
```
